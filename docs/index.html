Outside

The simple way to tell friends where you're headed in Berlin — and see who's coming.

Get it on Google Play Support
🛝

Pick a spot

Search any place — a playground, park, café, library — or add your own with a tap.

📣

Broadcast it

Let friends know you're heading there now or soon. They get a gentle nudge.

🙌

See who's in

Friends say "I'm coming," "maybe," or "can't" — so plans happen without the group-chat back-and-forth.

👨‍👩‍👧

Solo or family

Use it as yourself, or broadcast together as a family. Friends, not strangers — requests need accepting.
